# Modular Extrusion Pedalboard System

## Overview

Every pedalboard in the market still requires stick and peel velcro, zipties, or custom backplates you buy for each and every pedal (good luck if you get custom pedals) that all absolutely require some kind of semi-permanent modification to your precious pedals. The SOLIDGRIP Pedalboard system replaces all of this with a completely noninvasive mechanical fixturing system, and uses standard aluminum extrusion profiles to create a modular pedalboard system design to work with you.

---

## System Topology

### 1. Chassis
* **Aluminum Extrusion Rails:** Constructed using standard metric 2040 (or 2020) aluminum extrusions, providing high torsional rigidity, low weight, and universal T-slot modularity.
* **Hardwood End Cheeks:** Custom CNC-machined wooden side cheeks provide structural anchoring and frame ergonomics.
* **Integrated Cable Clearance:** Rails are arranged with 40 mm spacing between profiles to route audio, power, and patch cables beneath the playing surface.
* **Integrated Mains Power:** Incorporates an IEC power junction module routed through the chassis for clean internal power distribution.

### 2. SOLIDGRIP Clamps
Pedals are secured mechanically without adhesives, preserving enclosure finishes and resale value.
* Each clamp features a fixed base secured to the rail via a drop-in T-nut and a moving jaw riding along two 45° beveled sliding interfaces.
* Tightening the locking screw forces the moving jaw down the 45° ramp, applying sideways and downwards clamping force to completely grab your pedals securely and hold them against the extrusion framing.
* The clamp jaws utilize 15 × 10 mm NBR rubber pads to provide tight grip and surface cushioning without marring painted or powder-coated enclosures.
* Designed for additive manufacturing in ABS, with print layer grain aligned parallel to sliding faces to minimize wear and prevent binding.

## Fastening & Mechanical Specifications

| Parameter | Specification | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Rail Profile** | 2040 / 2020 Metric Aluminum Extrusion | Fits standard drop-in M4 T-nuts |
| **Drive Hardware** | M4 × 0.7 SHCS | Class 8.8 or stainless steel |
| **Jaw Travel** | 3.0 mm (X-axis) | Accommodates enclosure tolerances |
| **Contact Interface** | 15 mm × 10 mm NBR Rubber Pad | Protects finish; provides high static friction |
| **Moving Jaw Recommended Torque** | 0.5 – 0.6 N·m | Protects embedded hex nut in printed ABS |
| **Rail Anchor Recommended Torque** | 1.0 – 1.2 N·m | Direct interface into metal drop-in T-nut |
| **Lateral Hold Force** | ~300 N per clamp (~1200 N per 4-clamp pedal setup) | Prevents lateral slip under use |
| **Friction Holding Force (Y-axis)** | ~840 N total (per 4-clamp setup, NBR rubber) | Holds up to aggressive stomps |
